### 🧂 Calcium (Ca) Deficiency — Key Symptoms

- Young leaves show curling, tip burn, and wrinkling.
- Leaf margins turn brown and dry (necrosis).
- Root tips and growing points die back due to poor cell division.
- Fruits show blossom-end rot (tomato, chilli), bitter pit (apple), and cracking (banana).
- Poor leaf expansion, weak stems, and reduced root growth.

---

### 🌍 Soil / Environmental Causes of Calcium Deficiency

- Acidic soils (pH < 5.5) or highly sandy soils.
- Excess salts or Sodium accumulation reduces Calcium uptake.
- Continuous heavy rainfall or poor drainage leaches calcium.
- High Potassium (K) or Magnesium (Mg) application interferes with Ca absorption.
- Low transpiration crops (banana, grapes) show more symptoms in humid climates.

---

### 🌱 Soil Application (Long-Term Correction)

- Apply **Gypsum (Calcium Sulphate)** or **Lime (CaCO₃)** depending on soil pH.
- **Gypsum dose:** 200–400 kg per acre (500–1000 kg/ha) in neutral or alkaline soils.
- **Lime dose:** 150–300 kg per acre (375–750 kg/ha) for acidic soils (pH < 6.0).
- Mix with compost/FYM and apply uniformly; avoid direct contact with young roots.
- Improves soil structure, drainage, and long-term calcium availability.

---

### 💧 Foliar Spray (Quick Relief)

- **Calcium Nitrate (CaNO₃) 1% spray** for immediate uptake.
- Preparation: **10 g per litre of water**, add mild wetting agent.
- Spray during **cool hours (evening preferred)** to avoid leaf scorch.
- Repeat spray **2–3 times at 7–10 day intervals** depending on severity.
- Suitable for fruiting crops like banana, tomato, chilli, guava, citrus.

---

### ⚠️ Precautions & Compatibility

- Never mix **Calcium Nitrate** with **Sulphates or Phosphates** (causes white precipitate).
- Avoid spraying during **hot sunny hours** — increases leaf burn risk.
- Maintain moisture before spraying; avoid spraying on drought-stressed plants.
- Excess Potassium (MOP) reduces Calcium uptake — balance K application.

---

### 🍌 Crop-Specific Notes (Banana, Tomato, Grapes, Citrus)

- **Banana:** Fruit cracking and deformed fingers → Calcium Nitrate 1% at early fruit development stage.
- **Tomato/Chilli:** Blossom-end rot → Regular Ca spray during fruit setting stage.
- **Grapes:** Berry softening and cracking → Apply CaNO₃ + Boron (0.1%) with caution.
- **Citrus:** Leaf curl and weak rind → Prefer Calcium Nitrate foliar spray + lime in soil for long-term improvement.
