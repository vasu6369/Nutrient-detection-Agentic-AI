### 🟤 Manganese (Mn) Deficiency — Key Symptoms

- **Interveinal chlorosis** on young and recently matured leaves (not fully new like iron).
- Small brown/black specks or **“leaf speckling”** between veins.
- Veins remain greener than the leaf lamina but not as sharp as iron deficiency.
- Leaves may turn pale grey, crinkled, and brittle under severe deficiency.
- Reduced leaf size and weak root growth lead to stunting.

📌 Difference from Iron Deficiency:  
- **Iron → strong chlorosis on youngest leaves with clear green veins**  
- **Manganese → chlorosis + brown specks, on young but not the newest leaves**

---

### 🌍 Soil & Environmental Causes

- High pH or alkaline soils (pH > 7.5).
- Calcareous soils with excess lime reduce Mn availability.
- Sandy soils low in organic matter.
- Excess application of **Phosphorus or Iron** may suppress Mn uptake.
- Waterlogged or poorly aerated soils lead to oxidized, unavailable Mn forms.

---

### 🌱 Soil Application (Long-Term Correction)

- Apply **Manganese Sulphate (MnSO₄)** to the soil.
- Recommended dose: **8–12 kg per acre (20–30 kg/ha)** depending on deficiency level.
- For alkaline soils, mix MnSO₄ with **compost/FYM** and apply in moist soil.
- Avoid broadcasting on completely dry soil; irrigate lightly after application.
- Organic matter addition (compost) helps keep Mn in available form.

---

### 💧 Foliar Spray (Quick Relief)

- **MnSO₄ 0.5% foliar spray** recommended.
- Preparation: **5 g MnSO₄ per litre of water**, add mild wetting agent.
- Spray during **morning or evening**; avoid high sunlight to reduce burning.
- Repeat spray **after 10–15 days** depending on severity.
- Works quickly for **banana, paddy, pulses, and vegetables** showing speckled leaves.

---

### ⚠️ Precautions & Compatibility

- Do **not mix** MnSO₄ spray with **alkaline pesticides** (lime sulfur, Bordeaux mixture).
- Avoid spraying with strong Copper formulations — can reduce Mn absorption.
- Maintain proper drainage in paddy and other waterlogged crops.
- Excess Mn (rare) causes **brown patches and toxicity** — avoid overuse in acidic soils.

---

### 🍌 Crop-Specific Notes (Banana, Paddy, Soybean, Citrus)

- **Banana:** Light yellow leaves with brown specks → 0.5% MnSO₄ foliar spray + compost addition improves response.
- **Paddy/Rice:** Common in poorly drained/alkaline fields → soil MnSO₄ + drainage correction recommended.
- **Soybean & Pulses:** Speckled chlorosis on young leaves → foliar spray effective during early growth stage.
- **Citrus:** Patchy yellowing with green veins → combine soil MnSO₄ + organic matter to maintain long-term availability.
