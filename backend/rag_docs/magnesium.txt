### 🟢 Magnesium (Mg) Deficiency — Key Symptoms

- **Interveinal chlorosis** (yellowing between veins) on **older or lower leaves first**.
- Leaf veins remain dark green while the lamina turns pale yellow.
- Margins of leaves may turn reddish-purple under severe deficiency.
- Leaves may curl upward or show a tiger-stripe pattern in some fruit crops.
- Premature leaf shedding reduces photosynthesis and yield.

📌 Difference from Iron Deficiency:  
- **Magnesium → affects old leaves first**  
- **Iron → affects young leaves first**

---

### 🌍 Soil & Environmental Causes

- Sandy, acidic soils (pH < 6.0) with heavy leaching.
- High Potassium (MOP) or Calcium application competes with Magnesium uptake.
- Prolonged rainfall or irrigation leaches Mg from sandy soils.
- Low organic matter reduces Mg availability.
- Excess ammonium fertilizers may suppress Mg uptake in crops.

---

### 🌱 Soil Application (Long-Term Correction)

- Apply **Magnesium Sulphate (MgSO₄)** to the soil.
- Recommended dose: **20–40 kg per acre (50–100 kg/ha)** depending on severity.
- Mix with **FYM/compost** and apply uniformly around plant root zone.
- Split application helps in sandy soils or high rainfall regions.
- In acidic soils (pH < 6.0), apply **Dolomite Lime** for long-term improvement.

---

### 💧 Foliar Spray (Quick Relief)

- **MgSO₄ 2% foliar spray**.
- Preparation: **20 g MgSO₄ per litre of water**, add mild wetting agent.
- Spray during **cool hours (morning/evening)** for better absorption.
- Repeat **after 10–12 days if symptoms persist**.
- For banana, citrus, grapes → multiple sprays during fruit development stage help prevent yellowing.

---

### ⚠️ Precautions & Compatibility

- Do **not spray** MgSO₄ with **Bordeaux mixture or highly alkaline pesticides**.
- Avoid mixing with highly concentrated Potassium fertilizers (KCl/MOP) in the same spray tank.
- Maintain proper soil moisture; avoid foliar application on drought-stressed plants.
- Excessive Potassium can **worsen Mg deficiency** — balance fertilizer use.

---

### 🍌 Crop-Specific Notes (Banana, Citrus, Coconut, Tomato)

- **Banana:** Lower leaves turn yellow with green midribs → 2% MgSO₄ spray during bunch development stage is effective.
- **Citrus:** Yellow tiger stripes on old leaves → combine soil MgSO₄ + 2% foliar spray for quick correction.
- **Coconut:** Leaf margin yellowing on older fronds
