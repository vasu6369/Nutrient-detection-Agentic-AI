### 🔎 Boron Deficiency — Key Symptoms

- Thick, brittle young leaves; leaf cracking in severe cases.
- Shoot tips may die; stunted growth.
- Poor flowering and fruit set; malformed fruits.
- Fruit cracking common in banana, mango, and guava.

### 🧪 Soil Conditions Causing Boron Deficiency

- Sandy soils or low-organic matter soils.
- Highly alkaline soils (pH > 7.5).
- Long dry periods or drought, followed by irrigation.
- Excess application of Calcium or Potassium reduces Boron uptake.

---

### 🌿 Soil Application (Long-Term Correction)

- Apply **Borax (10% B)** or **Solubor (20% B)**.
- **Recommended dose: 1.5–2 kg per acre (3–5 kg/ha).**
- Always **mix thoroughly with sand or compost** before applying.
- Uniform broadcasting is important to prevent toxicity.
- **Do NOT apply near seed or young roots.**

---

### 💧 Foliar Spray (Quick Fix)

- Use **Boric Acid 0.2% spray**.
- **Preparation:** 2 g boric acid per litre of water.
- Spray during **cool hours (morning/evening)** to avoid leaf burn.
- Repeat **after 15–20 days** if symptoms persist.
- Add a mild **wetting agent** for better leaf absorption.

---

### 💡 Precautions & Toxicity Warning

- Boron is **highly toxic in excess** — avoid overuse.
- Maintain proper soil moisture — **never spray on drought-stressed plants**.
- Keep **10–15 days gap** between Boron spray and other strong fertilizers.

---

### 🍌 Crop Notes (Banana, Grapes, Coconut, Mango)

- **Banana:** Cracked fruits & stiff leaves → prefer foliar 0.2% + mild irrigation before spray.
- **Grapes:** Poor berry set → spray pre-bloom stage.
- **Mango/Coconut:** Basal application mixed with compost gives better flowering response.
