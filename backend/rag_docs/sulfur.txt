### 🟡 Sulphur (S) Deficiency — Key Symptoms

- Light yellowing (pale green) on **young leaves first** (immobile nutrient).
- Uniform yellowing across the leaf (not between veins like Mg).
- Thin, small, narrow leaves with reduced leaf size.
- Weak stems and delayed maturity; stunted growth.
- In oilseed crops (mustard, groundnut, sesame), deficiency reduces **oil content**.
- Reduced aroma, flavor compounds in onion, garlic, and spices.

📌 Difference from Nitrogen Deficiency:
- **Sulphur → pale yellow on young leaves first**
- **Nitrogen → yellowing starts on older/lower leaves first**

---

### 🌍 Soil & Environmental Causes

- Sandy soils low in organic matter.
- Heavy rainfall that washes away sulphate ions (SO₄²⁻).
- Excess phosphorus or nitrogen may mask sulphur deficiency symptoms.
- Continuous cropping without organic manure application.
- Low sulphur in irrigation water (RO/borewell water in some regions).
- Soils with low microbial activity → less sulphur mineralization.

---

### 🌱 Soil Application (Long-Term Correction)

- Apply **Gypsum (Calcium Sulphate)** or **Elemental Sulphur**.
- **Gypsum dose:** 20–30 kg per acre (50–75 kg/ha).
- Mix gypsum with **FYM/compost** and apply near the root zone, not touching the stem.
- For acidic soils (pH < 6.0), prefer **Elemental Sulphur** for slow release.
- Oilseed crops (mustard, groundnut) especially benefit from sulphur application.

---

### 💧 Foliar Spray (Quick Fix)

- **2% Ammonium Sulphate (AS) foliar spray** recommended.
- Preparation: **20 g ammonium sulphate per litre of water**, plus mild wetting agent.
- Spray in **morning or evening**, avoid hot sun.
- Repeat after **12–15 days** if symptoms persist.
- Works effectively in **onion, garlic, cabbage, mustard, and groundnut**.

---

### ⚠️ Precautions & Compatibility

- Do **not mix** foliar sulphur sprays with **lime or alkaline pesticides** (causes precipitation).
- Avoid using elemental sulphur directly on young seedlings — can cause burn.
- Maintain good soil moisture before foliar spray for proper absorption.
- Balanced Nitrogen is needed — excess nitrogen without sulphur reduces protein quality.

---

### 🌾 Crop-Specific Notes (Groundnut, Mustard, Onion, Garlic)

- **Groundnut:** Pale yellow young leaves and reduced pegging → gypsum 30 kg/acre at early flowering + soil moisture maintenance.
- **Mustard:** Low oil content and light-colored leaves → soil gypsum + foliar ammonium sulphate improves yield and oil recovery.
- **Onion & Garlic:** Reduced aroma/flavor and thin bulbs → sulphur enhances flavour oils; elemental sulphur helps with long-term correction.
- **Cabbage & Cauliflower:** Poor head formation → combine gypsum in soil + 2% ammonium sulphate foliar spray.
