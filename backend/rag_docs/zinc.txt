### 🔵 Zinc (Zn) Deficiency — Key Symptoms

- **Shortened internodes** (reduced plant height), known as “rosetting”.
- Younger leaves show **interveinal chlorosis with whitish stripes**.
- Leaves may become small, narrow, and distorted.
- Brown spots or necrotic patches appear on leaf tips/margins under severe deficiency.
- Poor flowering & delayed maturity; grain/crop quality reduced.
- In rice, wheat, maize: causes **“Khaira disease”** (grey/brown patches on seedlings).

📌 Difference from Iron Deficiency:
- Both affect young leaves, but **zinc causes small distorted leaves + short plants**, while **iron causes yellowing without leaf size reduction**.

---

### 🌍 Soil & Environmental Causes

- **Alkaline soils (pH > 7.5)** or calcareous soils with high bicarbonates.
- Sandy soils low in organic matter.
- Continuous application of **phosphorus fertilizers (DAP/SSP)** reduces Zn uptake.
- Flooded paddy fields convert Zn to unavailable form.
- Excess water or poor drainage in rice increases zinc deficiency risk.
- Low microbial activity reduces Zn release from organic matter.

---

### 🌱 Soil Application (Long-Term Correction)

- Apply **Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO₄·7H₂O)** to soil.
- Recommended dose:  
  **10–12 kg per acre (25–30 kg/ha)** for crops like paddy, wheat, maize, banana.
- Mix with FYM/compost to improve availability and reduce fixation in alkaline soils.
- Avoid applying along with heavy doses of phosphorus on the same day.
- For sustained availability in acidic soils, **Zinc Oxide (ZnO)** can also be used.

---

### 💧 Foliar Spray (Quick Relief)

- **Zinc Sulphate 0.5% + Lime 0.25%** foliar spray is recommended.
- Preparation per litre:  
  **5 g ZnSO₄ + 2.5 g lime**, dissolve separately and mix.
- Lime reduces leaf burn and improves absorption.
- Repeat **2–3 sprays at 10–12 day intervals** if deficiency persists.
- Effective for **rice, maize, wheat, banana, citrus, grapes, and vegetables**.

---

### ⚠️ Precautions & Compatibility

- Do **not spray zinc sulphate** without lime — can cause leaf burn.
- Avoid mixing zinc spray with phosphatic fertilizers or strong copper-based fungicides.
- Maintain good soil moisture before application; **avoid spraying on drought-stressed plants**.
- Do not apply zinc sulphate directly to seed or root in concentrated form (causes toxicity).

---

### 🌾 Crop-Specific Notes (Rice, Maize, Banana, Groundnut)

- **Rice (Khaira Disease):** Grey/brown leaf patches & stunting → soil ZnSO₄ application + drainage improvement is essential.
- **Maize/Wheat:** Short plants with striped leaves → apply ZnSO₄ soil application early + foliar spray at knee-high stage.
- **Banana:** Small, narrow leaves & poor bunch development → foliar Zn + FYM + split ZnSO₄ soil dose recommended.
- **Groundnut:** Pale top leaves & weak pods → soil ZnSO₄ before pegging stage improves kernel quality.
